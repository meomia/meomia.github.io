About

A bit about me

I'm Dariia, an MSc Software Design student at the IT University of Copenhagen. My path to data science wasn't straightforward — I first discovered a passion for data-driven decision making while running my own Scandinavian food supplements web-shop in Ukraine, where I tracked sales patterns, conversion rates, and customer behaviour in Excel. When the war forced me to close the business and move to Denmark with my husband in 2022, I decided to turn that curiosity into a career.

I started from scratch — completing a data analytics course at ReDI School in Copenhagen, catching up on mathematics, and eventually being admitted to the MSc programme at ITU. I'm now looking for a part-time role in Copenhagen where I can apply my skills in a real-world product or data environment.
